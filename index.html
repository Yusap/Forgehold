Recipes

Recipes are discovered by exploring. Crafting spends items collected from your lands (see Inventory) — not Wood/Stone/Gold currency. A recipe can be reused as many times as you can afford. Costs show as "have/needed".

Crafting Station

Only one crafted item can sit here at a time — attach it to a land or discard it before crafting another. Items can be attached to any land, but each affix only helps if its own target matches. Modifying an item at the Crafting Station costs drop items of the resource it's bound to, escalating in tier each time (see the Glossary for full detail).
